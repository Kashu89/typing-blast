// pro tips

Type Like a Pro

Evidence-backed techniques used by professional typists and competitive keyboarders.

01
Home Row Position
Keep fingers resting on ASDF (left) and JKL; (right). Return to this position after every keystroke — it's the foundation of fast typing.
02
Look Ahead, Not Down
Read 2–3 words ahead of what you're typing. Train your brain to buffer the next word while your fingers handle the current one.
03
Accuracy Before Speed
Speed is a byproduct of accuracy. Correct muscle memory takes weeks to form — wrong habits take months to unlearn. Slow down first.
04
Fix Weak Fingers
Use the heatmap to spot cold keys. Your pinky and ring fingers are usually the weakest. Target them specifically with drills.
05
Rhythm Over Bursts
Consistent rhythm beats sprint-pause-sprint. Think of it like music — a steady beat is faster than unpredictable rushes.
06
Practice Daily (10 min)
10 minutes per day beats 1 hour on weekends. Spaced repetition is how motor skills get wired into the brain permanently.
